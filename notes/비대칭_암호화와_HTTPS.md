## Asymmetric Encryption

Asymmetric encryption is an encryption method in which the encryption key and the decryption key are different from each other.

## HTTPS

[[HTTPS]] (HyperText Transfer Protocol Secure) is a protocol that secures [[HTTP]] communication with [[TLS]].

---

## 한국어

### 비대칭 암호화

비대칭 암호화(Asymmetric Encryption)는 암호화 키와 복호화 키가 서로 다른 암호화 방식이다.

### HTTPS

[[HTTPS]](HyperText Transfer Protocol Secure)는 [[HTTP]] 통신을 [[TLS]]로 보호한 프로토콜이다.
